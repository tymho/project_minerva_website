1. App Use and Photo Access

To do its job, Chunky Time (“the App”) asks iOS for permission to access your photo library. You choose whether to grant (1) full access, (2) limited access to selected photos, or (3) no access at all. Photo access levels can be changed at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Chunky Time.

After you grant the App photo access:

  • TinyCone and third parties do NOT receive your photos, videos, or embedded metadata (i.e. dates and location tags embedded by your camera) .
  • Your photos, videos, and their metadata are read and processed entirely on your device.
  • Albums, groupings, and any organizational data the App creates are stored locally on your device.
  • Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the App deletes the App’s local data along with it. We have no copy to delete and no way to recover it for you.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect is either automatically sent to us by your device(s) in the course of using the App or is limited information that you have voluntarily and knowingly provided us.

2.1. Automatically Collected Information

Through the use of the App, TinyCone automatically collects the below information for App analytics and improvements:

Device Information

  • Technical information about the device(s) used to access the App
  • Crude location (time zone, country, continent) and date of the App’s download
  • Mobile device identifiers, such as Apple IDFA - which are technical identifiers developed by mobile operating systems for advertising purposes which remain under your control and can be reset or erased at any time in your device settings)

Diagnostics Information

  • Two anonymous App installation IDs, one for performance and another for subscription tracking, not tied back to any identifying information
  • Crash reports (stack traces, error messages, device state at time of crash)

App Usage Information

  • Data in relation to preferences and activities on the App, such as your App settings configurations and interactions with the App
  • Subscription status (whether a subscription is active, your plan type, transaction identifiers from Apple)

2.2. Voluntarily Provided Information

Aside from granting photo access, you are not required to provide Chunky Time additional access or information in order to use the App. We collect the below information ONLY if you choose to provide it to us in the below scenarios:

When Using the Feedback Board

  • The username / display name provided when using the Feedback Board in the App. This does NOT have to be your real / legal name.
  • The device vendor identifier provided when using the Feedback Board in the App. This links anonymous user sessions.

When Using the Contact Support Form

  • The email and additional diagnostic details you choose to provide through the in-app “Contact Support” button.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

TinyCone does not collect via third party service providers or directly through the App:

  • Your photos, videos, or any thumbnails, embeddings, or content derived from them.
  • Your name, address, phone number, date of birth.
  • Your precise location. The App does not request location permission.
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • Payment card numbers or billing details — those go to Apple, not to us.
  • Your Apple Account email or identifier.
  • Personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Any other sensitive personal information (such as your social security or passport number, driver’s license, financial account credentials, biometric data, etc.)

4. Who We Share Information With and Why

Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party services for: usage analytics, crash and diagnostic reports, subscription processing and management, feedback management, and chunkytime.app (the “Site”) management. These services may access the limited data described in Section 2.1 solely when performing the above tasks for the purposes we specify. We do not give them permission to disclose or use any of our data for any other purpose.

We do not otherwise share or sell information to third parties, including marketers (see Section 5).

Per Legal Requirements

We may preserve and/or disclose information where necessary and appropriate to comply with legal process, such as for a valid government or law enforcement request, or as required by law.

We may also disclose information under the good faith belief that such action is necessary to protect and defend the rights, safety, and/or property of TinyCone, LLC; to protect the safety of the public and any person; to prevent a crime; or to prevent what we reasonably believe to be illegal, legally actionable, or unethical activity.

5. How Long We Keep Information

We do not store any personal information, user photos, and videos. For app usage data, we keep it for one year.

6. Tracking and Advertising

The App contains no advertising and no third-party ad SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, so the App does not present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals because we do not track across sites; on our Site we honor Global Privacy Control signals where technically feasible.

7. Security

We limit any data we collect to the minimum amount necessary to operate the App. The data stored locally on your device by the App is protected by iOS, including device-level encryption when you have a passcode or biometric lock enabled. Information sent to our Service Providers travels over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.
