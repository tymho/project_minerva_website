  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use it for cross-app tracking.

1.Your photos and photo library

To do its job, the App asks iOS for permission to access your photo library. You choose whether to grant full access, limited access to selected photos, or no access at all, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → TinyCone.

When you grant access:

  • Your photos, videos, and their metadata (such as dates and location tags embedded by your camera) are read and processed entirely on your device.
  • Albums, groupings, and any organizational data the App creates are stored locally on your device.
  • None of this is transmitted to us or to any third party.

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting the App deletes the App’s local data along with it. We have no copy to delete and no way to recover it for you.

3.What we do not collect

  • Your photos, videos, or any thumbnails, embeddings, or content derived from them.
  • Your name, address, phone number, or date of birth.
  • Your precise location. The App does not request location permission.
  • Your contacts, calendar, microphone, or health data.
  • Payment card numbers or billing details — those go to Apple, not to us.
  • Your Apple Account email or identifier.

4.How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the App and Site;
  • diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and monitor performance and stability;
  • understand which features are used so we can decide what to build next;
  • deliver and restore paid subscription features;
  • send you the launch announcement if you joined the waitlist; and
  • comply with law and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

5.Legal bases (EEA/UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process personal data on these legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — for crash reporting, security, and basic product analytics, to keep the App working and improve it.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the subscription features you purchased.
  • Consent — for waitlist emails, and for any analytics that require consent under applicable law. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — where we must retain or disclose data by law.

6.Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share the limited data described above only with service providers who process it on our behalf, under contract, and only for the purposes we specify:

  • Apple — processes subscription purchases and provides App Store analytics.
  • [ANALYTICS PROVIDER] — processes usage analytics and crash reports.
  • Google — stores waitlist emails submitted on the Site (Google Sheets / Apps Script).
  • GitHub — hosts this website (GitHub Pages), which processes standard server request logs including IP addresses.

We may also disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of TinyCone or others. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that transaction; we will notify you before your personal information becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

7.Tracking and advertising

The App contains no advertising and no third-party ad SDKs. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, so the App does not present Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. We do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals because we do not track across sites; on the Site we honor Global Privacy Control signals where technically feasible.

8.How long we keep information

  • On-device data (photos, albums, organization) — kept until you delete it or delete the App. We never hold a copy.
  • Analytics and crash data — retained for up to [e.g. 14 months], then deleted or aggregated so it no longer identifies a device.

9.Security

Data the App stores on your device is protected by iOS, including device-level encryption when you have a passcode or biometric lock enabled. Information sent to our service providers travels over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). We keep the data we collect to a minimum, which is itself the strongest protection we can offer. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10.Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Because we hold so little data, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Photos and on-device data: revoke photo access in iOS Settings, or delete the App.
  • Analytics: turn off analytics sharing in the App’s settings, and use Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements on iOS.
  • Waitlist email: email us and we will delete it, or use the unsubscribe link in any email we send.

For anything else, email privacy@tinycone.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law (generally 30 days, or 45 days in California). We may need to ask for information to verify your request. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Categories collected: identifiers (a random installation identifier; an email address if you joined the waitlist), internet or other electronic network activity information (feature usage, crash diagnostics), and commercial information (subscription status).
  • Sources: directly from you, and automatically from your device through the App and Site.
  • Business purposes: those listed in Section 4.
  • Categories disclosed to service providers: those listed in Section 6.
  • Sold or shared: none. We have not sold or shared personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not do so with the personal information of minors under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect or use sensitive personal information for purposes that would require a right to limit.
